Systematic Planning Strategy

The 1885 Divide

Research in Puerto Rico is split by the implementation of the Civil Registry on **January 1, 1885**.

  • Post-1885: Priority is *Registro Civil*. Very detailed, listing grandparents and places of origin.
  • Pre-1885: Priority is *Registros Parroquiales* (Catholic Church). Look for "Bautismos," "Matrimonios," and "Entierros."

Naming Conventions

Always record the double surname: [First] [Paternal] [Maternal].

  • Women keep their maiden names in records.
  • This is your greatest asset in tracking female lineages across centuries.

FS: Civil Registry

Births, Marriages, Deaths (1885-2001).

FS: Church Records

Catholic registers dating back to the 1600s.

PARES (Spanish Archives)

Colonial era documents and military records.
