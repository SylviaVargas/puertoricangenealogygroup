## Puerto Rican Genealogy Zoom Meeting

**Monday, January 11, 2027**

### Members' Sharing Night

Start the new year with an open community session. There is no guest speaker: this night belongs to the members. Bring a discovery from the past year, a brick wall you are stuck on, a tool or source that helped you, or a research goal for 2027.

This is a relaxed chance to get fresh eyes on a problem, celebrate a hard-won find, and hear what fellow researchers are working on.

### What We Will Discuss

- **New Discoveries**: Share a record, connection, or breakthrough from your recent research
- **Brick Walls**: Bring a problem you are stuck on, the group may have leads
- **2027 Research Goals**: What are you hoping to find or finish this year?
- **Open Q&A**: Questions for fellow researchers and discussion
