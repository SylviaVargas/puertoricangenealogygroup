All 78 municipalities with parish and civil registry information. Use this guide to identify where records were kept and how far back they extend before searching.

Civil registration began in 1885 for all municipalities existing before that year. For records before 1885, consult the parish. If parish records start after the founding year, earlier records are typically held in the mother town's parish.

Mother-Town Relationships

Before a new municipality existed, its residents recorded baptisms, marriages, and burials in the mother town's parish. If you cannot find a record in your town, check the mother town, especially for events before or near the founding year.

Towns with multiple mother towns appear under each parent. Founding year shown in parentheses.
