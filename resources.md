## Puerto Rican Genealogy Resources

A curated collection of archives, databases, and tools for researching Puerto Rican ancestry.

---

### Getting Started & Research Guides

New to Puerto Rican genealogy? These guides will help you plan your research and understand what records are available.

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| <a href="https://libguides.nypl.org/puertoricogenealogy/atnypl" target="_blank" rel="noopener">New York Public Library: Puerto Rican Genealogy Guide</a> | A comprehensive online research guide with starting points, record types, and links to additional resources. Includes research strategy, key record sets, and historical context. Great for beginners. |
| <a href="https://guides.loc.gov/puerto-rico-local-history-genealogy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Library of Congress: Puerto Rico Genealogy & Local History</a> | A broad research guide pointing to vital records, historical maps, and catalog listings. Excellent for planning research into vital and court records. |
| <a href="https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Puerto_Rico_Genealogy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">FamilySearch: Puerto Rico Genealogy Wiki</a> | Free, structured research guide on civil, parish, and census records specific to Puerto Rico. Explains what records exist and how to use them. |
| <a href="https://boricuagenes.com/getting-started/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BoricuaGenes: Getting Started</a> | 10 practical tips for beginning your genealogical journey with Puerto Rican roots. |
| <a href="https://latinogenealogyandbeyond.com/puerto-rico-link-list/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Latino Genealogy & Beyond: Puerto Rico Link Collection</a> | A curated list of external links and learning resources useful for Caribbean and Puerto Rican research. |

### Archives & Official Repositories

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| <a href="https://www.icp.pr.gov/archivo-general/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Archivo General de Puerto Rico</a> | The official repository for Puerto Rican historical records, including colonial documents, census records, and government archives. |
| <a href="https://www.salud.pr.gov/CMS/18" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Registro Demográfico de Puerto Rico</a> | Puerto Rico's vital records office for birth, marriage, and death certificates (1885-present). |
| <a href="https://www.archives.gov/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">U.S. National Archives (NARA)</a> | Federal records relating to Puerto Rico, including census records, military records, and naturalization files. |

### Genealogical Societies & Organizations

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| <a href="https://genealogiapr.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sociedad Puertorriqueña de Genealogía (SPG)</a> | Genealogy society based in San Juan, Puerto Rico. SPG hosts monthly conferences, workshops, and cultural visits. Events are shared on their <a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/spgpuertorico" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Facebook group</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@sociedadpr" target="_blank" rel="noopener">YouTube channel</a>. Members receive two annual editions of <em>Hereditas</em>, a journal featuring genealogical research articles, parish record compilations, and municipal archive transcriptions. |
| <a href="https://groups.io/g/PRGENEALOGY-NW" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Puerto Rican Genealogy Northwest</a> | Groups.io mailing list for Puerto Rican genealogy researchers. |
| <a href="https://www.ngsgenealogy.org/genealogy-courses/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Genealogical Society – Genealogy Courses</a> | Self-paced online genealogy courses for beginners through advanced researchers, covering research methods, documentation standards, DNA, land records, and specialized topics. |
