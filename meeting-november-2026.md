## Puerto Rican Genealogy Zoom Meeting

**Monday, November 9, 2026**

### A Conversation with Dr. Antonio Sotomayor

Dr. Antonio Sotomayor joined us for an informal interview, followed by an opportunity for questions from the group. The conversation was planned around his research methods, Puerto Rican archival sources, genetic genealogy, and the colonial family networks he has studied.

### Special Presentation

**Dr. Antonio Sotomayor** is a historian, genealogist, and Latin American and Caribbean Studies Librarian at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. His work combines traditional genealogical research, historical context, archival records, and genetic genealogy. Learn more on his website, <a href="https://www.asotomayor.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>asotomayor.com</em></a>.

**Resources to explore:**

- <a href="https://libreriaelcandil.com/products/familia-y-poder-en-puerto-rico" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>Familia y poder en Puerto Rico: los Sotomayor y sus parentelas entre los siglos XVI y XVIII</em></a>, his 2025 book on the Sotomayor family and its kinship networks in Puerto Rico from the sixteenth through eighteenth centuries
- <a href="https://www.asotomayor.com/_files/ugd/3948f8_ddf09a0cfad4481d8cfe322b471c60b0.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>"Blood and Paper"</em></a>, his study connecting documentary research with genetic evidence among several early Puerto Rican families
- <a href="https://www.asotomayor.com/bibliography" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>Genealogy research and selected bibliography</em></a>, a collection of archival, genealogical, historical, and genetic research sources
- <a href="https://experts.illinois.edu/en/persons/antonio-sotomayor" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>University of Illinois profile</em></a>, an overview of his scholarship and publications
