## Puerto Rican Genealogy Zoom Meeting

**Monday, December 14, 2026**

### César A. Zapata Lozada on Cabo Rojo Death Records (1833 to 1838)

Join us as César A. Zapata Lozada discusses his new book, <em>Parroquia San Miguel Arcángel de Cabo Rojo: Libro Noveno de Defunciones (1833–1838). Transcripción y Análisis</em>. The book transcribes and analyzes the death records of the San Miguel Arcángel parish in Cabo Rojo from 1833 to 1838.

### Special Presentation

**César A. Zapata Lozada** returns after his February 2026 presentation on municipal council records (*Actas de Cabildo*). Read more about that session on the <a href="{{ '/meeting-february-2026/' | relative_url }}">February 2026 meeting page</a>.
