## Puerto Rican Genealogy Zoom Meeting

**Monday, December 14, 2026**

### César Zapata-Lozada on His Upcoming Book

Join us as César Zapata-Lozada discusses his upcoming book, drawing on his research into the families and records of southwestern Puerto Rico. Book title and publication details will be announced here.

**César Zapata-Lozada** returns after his February 2026 presentation on municipal council records (*Actas de Cabildo*). Read more about that session on the <a href="{{ '/meeting-february-2026/' | relative_url }}">February 2026 meeting page</a>.
