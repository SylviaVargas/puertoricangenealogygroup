historical_overview: "Las Marías, founded in 1871 from territory belonging to Mayagüez, is a mountainous municipality in western Puerto Rico named for the indigenous María tree. Its Catholic parish holds records from 1842, making it one of the earlier record sets in the western region and a key resource for tracing families in the highland barrios. The community's identity was shaped by coffee cultivation and by the Grito de Lares uprising of 1868, both commemorated in the municipal coat of arms."
