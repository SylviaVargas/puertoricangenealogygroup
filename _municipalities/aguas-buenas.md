historical_overview: "Aguas Buenas was founded in 1838 by Francisco de Salas Torres, carved from the barrio of Aguabuena within Caguas, making Caguas its definitive pueblo matriz. Known as 'La Ciudad de las Aguas Claras' for its clear-water springs, the municipality comprises nine barrios and the town center. Catholic parish records begin in 1845 and civil registration starts in 1885, providing a solid documentary base for genealogical research into the families of this inland Central Puerto Rico community."
