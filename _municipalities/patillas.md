historical_overview: "Patillas was founded on October 31, 1811, on the southeastern coast of Puerto Rico, established on land donated by Doña Adelina Cintrón with the region's sugar cane economy as its foundation. The primary Catholic parish, Parroquia San Benito Abad y Santa Bárbara, holds records dating to 1810, predating the municipality's official founding. The town's name derives from the watermelons that once grew abundantly in the area, and its coat of arms preserves the town's dual patronal identity, agricultural heritage, and the religious symbolism of its founding era."
