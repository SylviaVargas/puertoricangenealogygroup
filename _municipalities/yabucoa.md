historical_overview: "Yabucoa was established on October 3, 1793, when territory was separated from neighboring Humacao, and is located in the fertile southeastern coastal valley of Puerto Rico. Known as La Ciudad del Nuevo Amanecer (The City of the New Dawn), the municipality built its colonial economy on cattle ranching and sugar production, later becoming the mother town of Maunabo in 1799. The main parish, Parroquia Santos Ángeles Custodios, holds baptismal records dating to 1813, marriage records from 1829, and burial records from 1837, with civil registration beginning in 1885. Researchers tracing Afro-Puerto Rican ancestry should note that Yabucoa's 1872 slave registers survive and are especially valuable for this historically sugar-producing region."
