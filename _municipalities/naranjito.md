Located in Puerto Rico's central mountain region, the municipality built its economy on coffee and tobacco agriculture. The parish of San Miguel el Arcángel anchors the town's genealogical record base, with registers beginning in 1831 documenting baptisms, marriages, and burials. Civil registration began in 1885 and provides records through the 20th century."
