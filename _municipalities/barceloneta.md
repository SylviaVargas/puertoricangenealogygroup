historical_overview: "Barceloneta, founded on July 1, 1881, by Bonocio Llenza Feliú, an immigrant from Barcelona, Spain, was carved from territory belonging to Manatí and briefly reannexed to it around 1900 before being restored as an independent municipality in 1911. The parish records of Nuestra Señora del Carmen are not yet fully indexed online, making civil registration from 1885 the most accessible source for most genealogical research. For events before 1885, researchers should contact the parish archive or the Archdiocese of San Juan directly."
