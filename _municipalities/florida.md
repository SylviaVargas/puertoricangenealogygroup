historical_overview: "Florida is Puerto Rico's youngest municipality, established on June 14, 1971, when it separated from Barceloneta, where it had existed as a barrio since 1881. Located in the karst region of northern Puerto Rico, it borders Arecibo, Barceloneta, Ciales, and Manatí. For genealogists, this history means that records for Florida-area families prior to 1971 are filed under Barceloneta in both civil registration and church records. The primary Catholic parish, Nuestra Señora de la Merced, was established in 1884 and holds sacramental records predating the municipality's independent status."
