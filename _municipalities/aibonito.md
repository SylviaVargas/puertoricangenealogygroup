Known as La Ciudad de las Flores (City of the Flowers) and Jardín de Puerto Rico, Aibonito occupies one of the highest elevations on the island, with a cool climate that favors flower cultivation and agriculture. The town's San José Parish holds records from 1831, and Aibonito's central-mountain location makes it a key research anchor for families who moved between Coamo, Cayey, Barranquitas, and neighboring municipalities throughout the 19th and 20th centuries."
