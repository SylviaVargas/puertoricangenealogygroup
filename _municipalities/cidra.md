historical_overview: "Cidra was founded in 1809 after separating from Cayey, its mother municipality, and is nestled in the central-eastern highlands of Puerto Rico. The town takes its name from the citron fruit historically cultivated in the region. The parish of Nuestra Señora del Carmen has anchored the community since 1818, leaving digitized baptismal, marriage, and burial records accessible through FamilySearch. Cidra's records are well preserved and productive for family historians tracing roots in the island's mountainous interior."
