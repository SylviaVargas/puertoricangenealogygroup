historical_overview: "Culebra is a small island municipality located northeast of Puerto Rico's main island, founded on October 27, 1880, by Cayetano Escudero as the settlement of San Ildefonso de la Culebra. Because of its late parish formation and geographic isolation, surviving Catholic records begin around 1891; researchers should also search Vieques and Fajardo records for earlier family events. Civil registration from 1885 supplements parish records and is the primary source for vital events after that date."
