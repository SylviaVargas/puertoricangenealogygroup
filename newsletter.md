### What Is in Your Free Starter Kit

When you subscribe, you receive five PDFs by email:

- **Module 1: Start With Yourself** — Five key questions to anchor your research before you touch an archive
- **Module 2: Talk to Living Relatives** — Interview preparation guide and question bank with a focus on migration and Puerto Rico
- **Module 3: Collect Basic Documents** — How civil and church records differ, how to search FamilySearch, and a Spanish genealogy glossary
- **Module 4: Keep a Research Log** — Research log format, how to document negative evidence, and Google Sheets templates
- **Quick Reference Card** — Printable one-page cheat sheet covering all four modules

---

### What to Expect Each Month

One email per month. Each issue includes:

**Upcoming meeting** — date, topic, and Zoom link so you never miss a session.

**New records alert** — we track when FamilySearch, Ancestry, and other platforms add Puerto Rico collections and report the most useful additions.

**Research tip of the month** — one practical, GPS-aligned strategy drawn from our course material and expert presentations.

**Community spotlight** — researcher discoveries, Ancestor Registry matches, and active surname callouts from our members.
