## Key Fields to Analyze (Preview)

When you access U.S. federal census records for Puerto Rico, look at:

- Name and age (compare across census years to catch discrepancies)
- Marital status
- Literacy (reading/writing in Spanish or English)
- Occupation and industry
- Municipality and barrio of residence
- Birthplace and parents' birthplace
- Migration status (for later census years)

> **Research strategy:** Always track a family across all available census years. Age reporting in Puerto Rican census records is frequently inconsistent — a person listed as 35 in 1910 may appear as 52 in 1920. Build a comparison log to document and analyze these discrepancies.
