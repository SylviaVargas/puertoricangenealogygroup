## A Note on AI and the GPS

AI tools can accelerate data organization and pattern recognition — but they do not replace genealogical judgment. Any AI-assisted conclusion must still meet all five elements of the Genealogical Proof Standard:

1. Reasonably exhaustive research
2. Complete and accurate citations
3. Thorough analysis of each source
4. Resolution of conflicting evidence
5. A soundly reasoned, coherently written conclusion

AI can help you organize data more efficiently. The reasoning, source evaluation, and final conclusion remain yours.

For more on applying AI to genealogical research, see the **[Teaching Genealogists AI](https://puertoricangenealogy.org/ai-genealogy/)** resources available on this website.
