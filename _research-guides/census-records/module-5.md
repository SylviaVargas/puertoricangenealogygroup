## Why This Module Matters

Island-wide censuses leave gaps — years between enumerations, municipalities with damaged records, and families who moved between census years. Municipal and barrio-level records can provide:

- Population data for specific barrios within a *pueblo*
- Local records created between major census years
- Evidence of household composition when census schedules are missing
