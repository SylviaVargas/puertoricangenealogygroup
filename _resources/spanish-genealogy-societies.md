## Spanish-Language Genealogy Societies

Genealogical societies in Spain and Latin America can be valuable partners for Puerto Rican research. Many Puerto Rican families trace ancestry to Spain, especially the Canary Islands and Cantabria. Others have family connections to other Caribbean and Latin American countries through migration, colonial ties, or the sugar trade.

This directory lists societies with verified, working websites as of March 2026.

---

#### Canary Islands

Many Puerto Rican families descend from Canary Islands settlers who arrived during the 18th and 19th centuries. These societies specialize in Canarian genealogical records.
