## Puerto Rican Genealogy Zoom Meeting

**Monday, May 11, 2026**

### From Cabo Rojo to San Francisco

Join us as Fred Rodríguez Segarra, board member of the Sociedad Puertorriqueña de Genealogía and longtime friend of our group, presents "From Cabo Rojo to San Francisco" — a journey tracing Puerto Rican roots across generations and geography. After the presentation, we will have a lively discussion and members will share their own brick walls and breakthroughs.

### Special Presentation

**Fred Rodríguez Segarra** is a board member of the Sociedad Puertorriqueña de Genealogía and a longtime friend of PuertoRicanGenealogy.org. His presentation traces a family's journey from the western coast of Puerto Rico to San Francisco, illustrating the research strategies and record types that can help you follow your own ancestors across borders.

### What We Will Discuss

- **Fred Rodríguez Segarra's Presentation**: "From Cabo Rojo to San Francisco" — tracing a Puerto Rican family across generations and geography

- **Breakthroughs and Discoveries**: Share a recent find or connection from your own research
- **Open Discussion**: Strategies for tracing Puerto Rican ancestors who migrated to the U.S. mainland
