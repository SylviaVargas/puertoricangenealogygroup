This Privacy Policy explains what information PuertoRicanGenealogy.org website (puertoricangenealogy.org) collects, how it is used, and your choices.

---

## What We Collect

### Analytics

This site uses two analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. Neither tool collects personal information or uses cookies.

**Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** Configured in cookieless mode with IP anonymization enabled. GA4 records page views, general geographic region (not precise location), device type, and referral source. No personally identifiable information is stored. For more information, see
<a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>Google's Privacy Policy</em></a>.

**GoatCounter:** An open-source, privacy-friendly analytics tool. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not track individuals across sites, and does not store personal data. For more information, see
<a href="https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>GoatCounter's Privacy Policy</em></a>.

### Contact

The site provides email links (`mailto:`) for contacting us. Any information you include in an email you send to us is handled according to standard email practices. We do not use server-side contact forms, and the site itself does not store any messages you send.

---

## What We Do Not Collect

- We do not require account registration or login.
- We do not use tracking cookies.
- We do not collect names, addresses, or payment information.
- We do not sell or share visitor data with third parties.

---

## Third-Party Links

This site contains links to external websites, including FamilySearch, Facebook, and other genealogical resources. Once you leave this site, you are subject to the privacy policies of those external sites. We are not responsible for their practices.

---

## Children's Privacy

This site is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
